About MBT

Building intelligent systems for a digital Kenya

MugoByte Technologies is a Kenya-based technology company building practical software and digital systems for businesses, farmers and traders.

MBT builds and hosts its own systems on MBT-owned servers. Full control over performance, security and how quickly issues get fixed. No third-party hosting platforms between us and our clients.

Eugene Mugo started MBT after seeing firsthand how difficult it was for local businesses to find good, affordable software that actually worked for their needs. Rather than complaining about the gap, he started filling it.

Today MBT covers web development, cloud hosting, AI systems, farm management and trading technologies. Each product was built from scratch based on real problems that real people were facing.
