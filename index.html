About MBT

Building Intelligent Systems
for a Digital Future

MUGOBYTE TECHNOLOGIES is a technology company based in Kenya, focused on building practical software and digital systems for businesses, farmers and traders.

MBT builds and hosts its own systems on MBT-owned servers. This means we have full control over how our products perform, how secure they are and how quickly issues get fixed. We do not rely on third party hosting platforms for our clients.

Eugene Mugo started MBT after seeing firsthand how difficult it was for local businesses to find good, affordable software that actually worked for their needs. Rather than complaining about the gap, he started filling it.

Today MBT covers web development, cloud hosting, AI systems, farm management and trading technologies. Each product has been built from scratch based on real problems that real people were facing.

Trading Strategies

Multi-Strategy AI Engine

📈

Trend Following

Identifies and rides strong market trends using moving averages, MACD, and momentum indicators to capture sustained market moves.

⚡

Scalping

High-frequency short-term trades that capture small price movements rapidly. Optimized for low-spread markets with tight execution.

🔄

Mean Reversion

Capitalizes on price deviations from statistical averages, entering positions when assets are statistically overbought or oversold.

🌊

Swing Trading

Medium-term position trading capturing price swings over hours or days using support/resistance levels and pattern recognition.

🚀

Momentum Trading

Enters positions aligned with strong price momentum, riding breakouts and continuation patterns with smart position sizing.

📊

Volatility Analysis

Uses ATR, Bollinger Bands, and volatility metrics to adapt strategy selection based on current market conditions.
