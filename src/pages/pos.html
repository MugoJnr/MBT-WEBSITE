System Features

Everything your business needs

Inventory Management

Track every product in real time. Set stock levels, get low-stock alerts, and manage categories and suppliers.

Barcode Support

Fast checkout with USB barcode scanners or your phone's camera. Works with all standard barcode formats.

Receipt Printing

Professional receipts with thermal printer support and templates carrying your business branding.

Sales Analytics

Daily, weekly and monthly reports. Profit analysis, expense tracking and your top-selling products at a glance.

Customer Records

Customer profiles, purchase history, credit accounts and loyalty tracking in one place.

Supplier Management

Track suppliers, manage purchase orders, record deliveries and keep supplier payment records current.

Multi-user access with role-based permissions — cashiers, managers and admins each see what they should.

Smart Notifications

Phone notifications for low stock, daily sales summaries, payment reminders and critical events.

Cloud Sync

Real-time cloud backup and synchronisation — your business data follows you to any device.
