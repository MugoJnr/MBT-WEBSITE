Outside MBT, Eugene works as a Technician at Edmus Organisation and writes for gocrossway.org. He loves cars, enjoys travelling, and finds that getting out and experiencing different things keeps his thinking fresh and his work grounded.

Passion & Vision

AI integration & the future of intelligence

Eugene's journey with AI started through automation and led to building the MBT AI Trading Bot. He is actively deepening his knowledge of AI tools, large language models and automation frameworks — looking for practical ways to bring them into MBT products.

He sees AI adoption in Africa — especially in agriculture, business and finance — as a real opportunity that can transform livelihoods at scale.

"I genuinely believe AI is one of the most important things happening in technology right now. My goal is to keep building my understanding and find practical ways to bring AI into what MBT builds, so that our clients benefit in real, useful ways. I feel like I am just at the beginning of this journey."

— Eugene Mugo, Founder & CEO, MugoByte Technologies
